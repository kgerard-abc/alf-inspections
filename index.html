    About this page. This page is published by Advocates for Better Care in Rhode Island (ABC-RI), an independent nonprofit advocacy organization. It is not produced, reviewed, or endorsed by the Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH) or any state agency, and it is not a substitute for RIDOH's own records.

    Every entry is drawn from RIDOH's own publicly posted inspection reports for licensed assisted living residences. The plain-language summaries are our own paraphrasing, written to be easier to read than the original regulatory language — they are not verbatim, and paraphrasing can introduce errors. The linked PDF for each entry is the official record; if anything here conflicts with that PDF, the PDF governs. Findings are marked "serious" when they involved actual harm to a resident, or a documented immediate risk of harm, as described in RIDOH's own report.

    Think something here is wrong? Email us at corrections@abc-ri.org with the facility name and what looks off. We review every correction request against the source document and fix confirmed errors promptly. If you disagree with how we've characterized a finding rather than with a factual error, we'll still note your response, but our summaries reflect RIDOH's own findings as written.
