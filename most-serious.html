Most serious findings at all residences

These are findings where a resident was seriously harmed, or where a failure was likely to cause serious injury, harm, or death. This is the same standard nursing home inspections call “immediate jeopardy.”

Assisted living rules in Rhode Island do not use the term “immediate jeopardy,” so there is no official list. This rating is our own judgment, applying the nursing home standard to RIDOH’s assisted living inspection reports.
